New in Suflo 3.4

Easing tab

Put one of 15 preset curves, or one you draw, between two of your own keyframes in Effect Controls. Premiere's scripting can't write Bezier handles, so Suflo writes the curve as keyframes on every frame (or every 2nd or 4th); your end keyframes stay as they are, and Undo removes only Suflo's keys. Editing and previewing are free; applying is Pro (3 free tries).

Follow-through and custom curves

Follow-through makes motion overshoot its landing keyframe a little and settle (Position, Scale, Rotation, Opacity). Transitions, auto zoom and motion presets get a curve picker, Speed Ramp gets a custom curve you draw, and any curve can be shared as a one-line code. Each tool keeps its own Pro gate and free tries.

Live-threshold silence cut

Auto Cut decodes the audio once, so the threshold sliders update the cut map instantly. Cut a selected clip, an In/Out range or the whole sequence; protected tracks such as background music are never split. After a cut, the captions in the panel follow the new edit without re-transcribing (captions already on the timeline are not changed).

AI bridge (MCP) · experimental

Lets Claude Desktop, Claude Code or another MCP client run Suflo's own actions, such as creating captions or analysing silences; cuts always land on a copy of the sequence after you confirm in the panel. It is off by default, accepts connections from this computer only, and turning it on needs Pro. Security model (Turkish, with an English summary).

New in Suflo 3.3

Caption Effects

The old style gallery is replaced by a builder. Start from six preset looks (Clean, Punchy, Vivid, Elegant, Glitch, Boxed), then set the entrance and highlight animations, word highlight, colors, bold or italic, lines and words on screen. The large preview is the real style engine output. Old saved styles and 3.2 style codes move to the nearest look.

Shadow, box and free position

Add a shadow and a background box, then drag the text anywhere on the preview. It snaps to the preset positions, and arrow keys nudge it. Style codes, Brand Kit and the Shorts Package carry the same settings.

7 new fonts

Bangers, Inter, Nunito, Poppins, Libre Caslon Display, Chakra Petch and Exo 2: 13 fonts in four categories, each shown in its own face. The new fonts cover every Turkish and Azerbaijani letter.

YouTube downloader

Paste a link, pick MP3, 720p, 1080p or 4K, and the file lands in a "Suflo İndirilenler" bin in your project. yt-dlp is downloaded on first use and verified with SHA-256. Only for content you have the right to download: your own videos, videos you have permission for, or licensed content. 3 free tries.

Native Premiere output (beta)

Pro users can choose Output › Native Premiere (no render) · Beta to add Caption Effects as Premiere's own clips, editable in Premiere (Premiere 2022 or later). It is new and still being tested, so the default stays the proven transparent video layer. Free tries always use the watermarked video layer.

New in Suflo 3.1

One-click Shorts Package

Pick viral moments and build them all at once: a 9:16 sequence per moment with styled captions, hook title, progress bar, call to action and your logo, plus YouTube Shorts, TikTok and Reels titles, descriptions and hashtags saved as .txt and .csv.

Podcast Mode

With one mic per speaker, Suflo switches to the camera of whoever is talking (2–4 speakers, optional wide shot). It works on a copy of the sequence, leaves audio untouched and colors each speaker’s captions in Caption Effects.

One-click cleanup & Viral Score 2.0

Text-based cut groups retakes and false starts and keeps one take, optionally matched to your script. Viral moments get an explained 0–100 score, never cut mid-sentence and come with 3 hook title ideas.

Brand Kit & style codes

Set your font, colors and corner logo once and they appear on Caption Effects and hook titles. Build an effect and share it as a one-line code anyone can paste into Suflo.

FAQ

Is Suflo Pro a subscription?

No. Suflo Pro is a one-time purchase: 749 TRY (≈ $19), tax added at checkout. Updates are included; there are no credits, minute limits or renewals.

Can I try Pro before buying?

Yes. Every code-based Pro tool gives you 3 free tries on your own footage. Tries never expire, and a try is used only when the job finishes. Styled caption layers made with a try carry a small suflo.app watermark, which is removed by re-rendering after you activate Pro. The Shorts Package, Podcast Mode and the content libraries (MOGRTs, SFX, presets) are Pro-only.

Why is checkout in Turkish lira?

Suflo is built in Türkiye and the store currently sells in TRY. The amount shown here is exactly what the button charges; your bank converts it (about $19). A USD checkout is planned.

How do I activate my license?

Paste the license key from your receipt into Settings › Suflo Pro in the panel. Pro content (MOGRTs, SFX, presets) then downloads automatically.

How does the invite program work?

Pro owners find a personal invite code under Settings in the panel. A friend who buys with it gets a discount at checkout, and reward packs download into the panel automatically after 1 and 3 invites. The free version has a share link that carries no personal information.
