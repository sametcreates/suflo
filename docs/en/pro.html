FAQ

Is Suflo Pro a subscription?

No. Suflo Pro is a one-time purchase: 749 TRY (≈ $19), tax added at checkout. Updates are included; there are no credits, minute limits or renewals.

Can I try Pro before buying?

Yes. Every code-based Pro tool gives you 3 free tries on your own footage, and a try is used only when the job finishes. Styled caption layers made with a try carry a small suflo.app watermark, which is removed by re-rendering after you activate Pro.

Why is checkout in Turkish lira?

Suflo is built in Türkiye and the store currently sells in TRY. The amount shown here is exactly what the button charges; your bank converts it (about $19). A USD checkout is planned.

How do I activate my license?

Paste the license key from your receipt into Settings › Suflo Pro in the panel. Pro content (MOGRTs, SFX, presets) then downloads automatically.
