Suflo Pro: the editing work, done for you

Auto Cut & one-click cleanup

Remove silences, filler sounds, repeats and long pauses. Text-based cut now also groups retakes and false starts and keeps one take of each: the last, the longest, the smoothest or the one you pick. Match takes to your script if you have one, and listen before you apply.

Viral Score 2.0

Every viral moment gets an explained 0–100 score (hook, stands alone, emotion, value, payoff). Set a genre, a count and what you are looking for. Clips never start or end mid-sentence; nudge them by one sentence and pick from 3 hook titles.

One-click Shorts Package

Add moments to a package and build them in one go: a 9:16 sequence per moment with styled captions (no re-transcribing), hook title, progress bar, call to action and your logo. Titles, descriptions and hashtags for YouTube Shorts, TikTok and Reels are saved to your project folder as .txt and .csv. An interrupted package picks up where it stopped.

Podcast Mode

When each speaker has their own mic, Suflo cuts to whoever is talking (2–4 speakers, optional wide shot). Pacing settings preview live, the edit lands on a copy of your sequence, and audio tracks are left alone. In Suflo Styles, each speaker gets their own caption color.

FAQ

Is Suflo really free?

Yes. The caption core (local transcription, editor, SRT/VTT/TXT export, applying captions to the Premiere caption track) is free and MIT-licensed, with no time limit. Suflo Pro is an optional one-time purchase for editing tools such as Auto Cut, viral Shorts and animated caption styles.

Which languages does it transcribe?

Suflo uses Whisper, which recognizes 99 languages, including English, Spanish, German, French, Portuguese, Arabic, Hindi, Japanese and Turkish. Set the caption language to Auto or pick one from the list.

Does my video get uploaded anywhere?

No. With the local engine, audio is transcribed on your computer and never leaves it. AI features such as viral moments send only the caption text, using your own free Groq key. If you choose the cloud engine, audio goes to Groq.

Is the English interface finished?

It is a beta. Almost every panel string is translated; a few messages may still appear in Turkish, and Premiere bin and marker names created by Suflo (Suflo Altyazi, Suflo Shorts) stay the same. Choose English (beta) on first launch or under Settings › Support › Interface language; switching needs no reload.

Which Premiere versions are supported?

Premiere Pro 2020 (14.4) and newer on Windows and macOS, including Premiere 25.6+ and 2026, where the panel lives under Window › Extensions (Legacy) › Suflo.

Why is the price shown in Turkish lira?

Suflo is built in Türkiye and checkout currently runs in TRY. 749 TRY (≈ $19) is charged once; your bank converts it. A USD checkout is planned, and this page will switch to it when it goes live.
