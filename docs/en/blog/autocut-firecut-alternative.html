AutoCut / FireCut Alternative, One-Time Price

Looking for an AutoCut or FireCut alternative without a subscription? We compare prices, features and gaps as of October 2026, with sources.

Adobe Premiere · Win/MacPrices: October 2026Updated: 2026-10-04

AutoCut and FireCut are two popular Premiere plugins that automate repetitive editing: silence removal, retake cleanup, captions and Shorts. Both are sold as monthly or yearly subscriptions. This page explains where Suflo, a one-time-price Premiere panel, is a good alternative and where it isn't.

Disclosure: this page was written by the team that builds Suflo, so we are not neutral. That is why competitor prices come from the plans their makers published as of October 2026, sources are listed at the end, and Suflo's gaps get their own section. Prices change; check the official pages before you decide.

Where Suflo is strong

  • One payment: buy Pro once, updates included. No monthly fee, credits or minute limits.
  • Local processing: speech is transcribed on your computer for captions and cuts; audio and video are not uploaded. AI steps send only text, with your free Groq key.
  • Non-destructive: text-based cut works on a copy sequence and Shorts are created as separate subsequences. Listen to a cut before you apply it.
  • Free core: captions in 99 languages, the editor and SRT/VTT export are free and open source with no time limit.
  • Multilingual from day one: fillers like "um", "uh" and "ııı" are detected, and Turkish casing rules (İ/ı) are handled correctly.

What Suflo doesn't have (yet)

The first five are features AutoCut or FireCut have and Suflo doesn't as of October 2026; the last two are Suflo's own limits:

  • No DaVinci Resolve support. Suflo works only in Premiere; AutoCut also supports Resolve.
  • No multicam podcast switching. Automatic camera switching by speaker is in AutoCut (AutoPodcast) and FireCut; it is on Suflo's roadmap.
  • No script-based retake removal. Pasting a script and removing retakes against it (AutoCut Repeat 2.0, FireCut text-based editing) is not in Suflo yet; text-based cut flags fillers, back-to-back repeats and long pauses.
  • No one-click first draft like FireCut's Magic Cut; you run the tools one by one.
  • No community preset library. AutoCut has 1000+ community caption presets; Suflo has built-in styles and lets you save your own.
  • AI steps need a key. Viral moments and chapter titles need a free Groq key, set up once in a minute.
  • English interface is in beta. A few messages may still appear in Turkish.

Which one fits you?

Choose AutoCut if you also edit in DaVinci Resolve, or a big community preset library or script-based retake removal is critical for you. Choose FireCut if you need a one-click first draft, multicam podcast switching or text-based editing today and a subscription suits you. Choose Suflo if you edit in Premiere, would rather pay once than subscribe, and want your footage to stay on your computer.

You can try all three without risk: AutoCut has a 14-day trial, FireCut a 7-day trial, and Suflo's free tier has no time limit plus 3 free tries of every code-based Pro tool (Auto Cut, zoom, enhance audio, styled captions…); content libraries are Pro-only. Frequently asked questions

Is Suflo Pro a subscription?

No. Suflo Pro is a one-time payment: 749 TRY (≈ $19) + tax as of October 2026. Updates are included; there are no monthly fees, credits or minute limits.

Does Suflo work in DaVinci Resolve?

No. Suflo is a panel for Adobe Premiere (Premiere Pro 2020 / 14.4 and newer). If you also edit in Resolve, a tool that supports both, such as AutoCut, may suit you better.

Are my videos uploaded to a server?

No. Speech is transcribed on your computer with local Whisper. AI steps such as viral moments send only caption text, with your free Groq key; audio and video are not sent.
